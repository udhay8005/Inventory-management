# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is maintained for the benefit of the Dakshin Vrindavan Gaushala
cow-care trust. We welcome contributions that improve the system and pledge to
keep all interactions respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the trust
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour and taking appropriate corrective action in response to any behaviour
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull
Requests, commit messages, code reviews) and whenever an individual is
representing the project in any public space.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the trust's contact inbox at **office.dakshinvrindavan@gmail.com**,
which is monitored by the project maintainer (**udhay8005**). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions
that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
